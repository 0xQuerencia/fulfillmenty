# Community Code of Conduct

Fulfillmenty is intended to be a respectful, constructive community for people improving international product sourcing.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith while discussing ideas.
- Give actionable feedback about work and data, not personal attacks.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and unwanted contact.
- Disclose relevant commercial or organizational conflicts when contributing data.
- Respect privacy and never publish addresses, payment details, or unredacted order evidence.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect people or the reliability of the project. Report an urgent safety or security issue through GitHub's private reporting tools rather than a public issue.
